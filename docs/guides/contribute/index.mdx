# Contribute to Blockly

Blockly is an open-source project with a strong community, and we welcome your
contributions.

## How you can help

-   [File bugs](/blockly/guides/modify/contribute/write_a_good_issue)
-   [File feature requests](/blockly/guides/modify/contribute/write_a_good_issue#feature_request)
-   [Fix bugs](/blockly/guides/modify/contribute/write_a_good_pr)
-   [Add tests](/blockly/guides/modify/web/unit-testing)
-   [Write codelabs](/blockly/guides/modify/contribute/write_a_codelab)
-   [Write plugins](/blockly/guides/modify/contribute/add_a_plugin)
-   [Answer questions](https://groups.google.com/g/blockly)

## Where to start

-   If you're new to open source development, or you want to get more background
    knowledge, head over to the
    [Getting Started contributing](/blockly/guides/contribute/get-started/)
    guides.
-   If you are ready to jump in, check out the
    [Contributing to Core](/blockly/guides/contribute/core/) or
    [Contributing to Samples](/blockly/guides/contribute/samples/) guides.

### Talk to us!

Help us focus our development efforts by telling us
[what you are doing with Blockly](https://goo.gl/forms/kZTsO9wGLmpoPXC02). The
questionnaire only takes a few minutes and will help us better support the
Blockly community.

Or join our
[newsgroup for developers](https://groups.google.com/g/blockly) and
say hello. Show us your prototypes early; collectively we have a lot of
experience and can offer hints which will save you time. Plus, we always love to
hear about new projects and use cases for Blockly!
